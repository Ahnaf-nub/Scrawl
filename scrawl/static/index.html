How to draw your level

Use a thick dark marker on plain paper, draw in landscape, and photograph it in good light.

  • Platform Any rectangle or thick line you can stand on.
  • Spikes Triangles hurt. You go back to the start.
  • Coin A small circle. Collect them all.
  • Goal One star. Reach it to win.
  • You A smiley face or the letter S marks where you start.
  • Enemy An X or a scribbled blob. It patrols back and forth.
Drawing a maze or a race track
  • Maze (top-down) Thick lines are walls. A smiley or S is the start, a star is the exit, a small key shape is a key (grab them all to unlock the exit), and an X is an enemy.
  • Race (top-down) Draw one closed loop for the road and a short line across it for the start. Add an arrow to set the direction, or the car goes clockwise.
